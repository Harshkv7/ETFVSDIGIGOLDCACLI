Know Your Costs

What are these charges?

Expense Ratio — annual fee deducted from your ETF every year.
▾

Pulled directly from the fund's NAV every day — you never see a bill, but your portfolio value quietly shrinks by this percentage every year. India's Gold ETFs charge between 0.50%–0.80% annually.

Nippon GoldBeES charges 0.79%/year. On ₹10 lakh, that's ₹7,900 gone in year one — and the deduction grows every year as your portfolio grows.
Tracking Error — the gap between gold's return and what your ETF actually pays.
▾

Even if gold rises 11%, your ETF may only return 10.75% — due to cash buffers, transaction costs, and rebalancing delays. India's Gold ETFs typically have 0.17%–0.30% tracking error per year.

SBI ETF Gold tracking error: ~0.20%. Every year, slightly less than gold. Invisible in your statement — you'd only catch it by comparing NAV to gold prices manually.
DigiGold Cost — 6% charged once, then zero charges forever.
▾

3% GST (government tax on gold) + ~3% platform spread, charged once at purchase. After that — nothing. No annual fee. No tracking error. Your remaining 94% compounds at the full gold return rate for as long as you hold.
