Know Your Costs

What are these charges?

Plain-language definitions. Tap any term for details.

What is Expense Ratio?
▾

The annual fee your ETF deducts from your portfolio every single year. It's pulled directly from the fund's NAV every day — you never see a bill, but your portfolio value quietly shrinks by this percentage every year. India's Gold ETFs charge between 0.50%–0.80% annually.

Nippon GoldBeES charges 0.79%/year. On ₹10 lakh, that's ₹7,900 gone in year one — and the deduction grows every year as your portfolio grows.
What is Tracking Error?
▾

The gap between gold's actual return and what your ETF actually delivered to you. Even if gold rises 11%, your ETF may only return 10.75% — due to cash buffers, transaction costs, and rebalancing delays. India's Gold ETFs typically have 0.17%–0.30% tracking error per year.

SBI ETF Gold tracking error: ~0.20%. Every year, slightly less than gold. Invisible in your statement — you'd only catch it by comparing NAV to gold prices manually.
What is the DigiGold cost?
▾

A one-time 6% deduction charged at purchase: 3% GST (government tax on gold) + ~3% platform spread. After that — nothing. No annual fee. No tracking error. Your remaining 94% compounds at the full gold return rate for as long as you hold.

The Math

How we calculate this

Transparent assumptions. No tricks. You can verify every number.

Gold ETFs Annual Cost

Each year, we deduct the combined expense ratio and tracking error from your portfolio value before applying the next year's returns.

This creates a compounding drag: the fee reduces your base, so every future return is applied to a smaller amount. Over 25 years, that compounds heavily against you.

Annual charges compound against your wealth — year after year after year.

DigiGold One-Time Cost

We apply a one-time 6% deduction upfront — covering the spread and 3% GST — to your initial investment.

After that, the remaining 94% compounds freely at the same gold return rate, with no further charges ever deducted.

The cost is paid once. Growth compounds forever.
