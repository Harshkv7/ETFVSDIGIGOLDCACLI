What are these charges?

Expense Ratio — annual fee deducted from your ETF every year. ▾

Pulled directly from the fund's NAV every day — you never see a bill, but your portfolio value quietly shrinks by this percentage every year.

Nippon GOLDBEES charges 0.79%/year. On ₹10 lakh, that's ₹7,900 gone in year one — and the deduction grows every year as your portfolio grows.
Tracking Error — the gap between gold's return and what your ETF actually pays. ▾

Even if gold rises 11%, your ETF may only return 10.75% — due to cash buffers, transaction costs, and rebalancing delays.

SBI ETF Gold tracking error: ~0.20%. Every year, slightly less than gold. Invisible in your statement.
Digital Gold Cost — 6% charged once at purchase. Nothing ever again. ▾

Approx 6% total cost — 3% GST at purchase, spread split across entry and exit. Nothing in between.
